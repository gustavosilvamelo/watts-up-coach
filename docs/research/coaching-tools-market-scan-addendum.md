# Coaching Tools Market Scan — Addendum

Extension of `coaching-tools-market-scan.md`. Focus: platforms conceptually adjacent to JOIN Cycling (amateur focus, adaptive plans, accessible pricing, simple UX) that the original scan did not cover.

**Source-quality rule.** This addendum inherits the market-scan trade-off: vendor pages and product help centers are accepted here (evidence-quality premise, `docs/premises.md`, scopes peer-review to physiology / health, not to commercial platform surveys). Blogs, forums, videos and social media were not used.

Captured: 2026-09-16.

---

## Platforms investigated

### Zwift Training Plans
- **Source:** https://www.zwift.com/training
- Exists. The official page is thin: describes plans as "flexible... fit around your schedule", with a focus on target events and FTP gain. **No explicit mention** of AI, adaptation to missed workouts or use of HRV/wellness. Included in the Zwift subscription (~US$ 19.99/month, single tier).
- **Audience:** indoor users who already pay for the virtual world.
- **Differentiation:** plans "free" bundled with the main gamified/social product — competes for attention, not for coaching quality.
- **Does not close any gap** identified in the original scan.

### Rouvy Training Plans
- **Source:** https://rouvy.com/training-plans
- Exists. Plans by segment (Masters 50+, beginners, club, TT, MTB, gravel, tri). The site **does not mention** adaptation, HRV, AI or ML — describes plans as "specialized" and "easy-to-follow" (i.e. **fixed plans**). Bundled with the Rouvy subscription.
- **Differentiation:** the only platform identified with a plan specifically for Masters 50+ (explicit demographic segmentation).
- **Confirms gap:** static content, no "why this workout", no cohort.

### Kinomap
- **Source:** https://www.kinomap.com (multiple variants tested)
- Exists but official content is extremely thin on every URL tested. Confirmed only as an indoor training platform with a library of real-video rides. No evidence of structured plans, adaptation or AI.
- Likely offering: "standalone workouts by video", not coaching.
- **Not a direct competitor of JOIN Cycling.**

### Peloton Cycling
- **Source:** https://www.onepeloton.com/bike
- Exists with important nuance: "**Peloton IQ**" was introduced — it "builds your plan" and "recommends your next step" — the first clear mention of an adaptive plan inside Peloton. The site does not specify data inputs (power? only cadence?) or algorithm. Bundled with All-Access Membership.
- **Audience:** mass fitness (not competitive cyclist).
- **Differentiation:** hardware + content + community integration, but locked to proprietary bike.
- **Low overlap with JOIN Cycling** (different audience, hardware lock-in), but signals that mass-market AI coaching is arriving via incumbents.

### FulGaz
- **Source:** https://fulgaz.com
- Exists. Mentions "our training plans" but **without detail** on adaptation, AI or data consumed. 14-day trial without card, AUD 14.99-21.99/month (pausable subscription + free family account are commercially unusual).
- **Audience:** cyclists and triathletes seeking realistic indoor.
- **Differentiation:** family account and pausable subscription (commercially interesting).
- **Does not close prescription / adaptation gaps.**

### Today's Plan
- **Source:** https://www.todaysplan.com.au
- **Shut down.** The site states "we appreciate the years of support". Remove from the active competitor set.

### TrainAsONE
- **Source:** https://www.trainasone.com
- Exists and is **relevant as a reference**, although exclusively running. Engine "Artemis 3" trained on 100M+ km of real data. The plan "rebuilds after every run", accounting for missed sessions, performance and fatigue. Consumes HR, perceived effort, running power, TSS, recovery. Sync with Garmin/Apple/Coros/Suunto/Polar/Stryd/Strava. 21-day trial. Claim: 75% of users hit a PR in 1 year.
- **This is the real benchmark for what "adaptive" means.** If a cycling platform still does not rebuild post-session, TrainAsONE shows it is possible and commercially viable.
- **Reference for benchmarking "adaptive" in cycling.**

### Coros Training App / Training Hub
- **Source:** https://coros.com
- Exists. Offers "structured programs" including cycling workouts, plus "COROS Training Hub" for planning/analysis. The site does **not** explicitly mention algorithmic adaptation, AI/ML or EvoLab (although EvoLab exists in the Coros ecosystem as load analysis). Plans appear to be "made by coaches" (curated, not generated). Free with a Coros watch.
- **Audience:** Coros watch users (serious endurance, price/value vs Garmin).
- **Differentiation:** free plans bundled with cheap hardware.
- **Does not appear truly adaptive.**

### Garmin Coach
- **Source:** https://www.garmin.com/en-US/garmin-coach/ (direct path returns 404; exists via connect.garmin.com)
- Exists inside the Garmin ecosystem but official content was inaccessible (404/403). Based on established public knowledge: covers primarily running (5K/10K/HM/M) with "adaptive plans by Greg McMillan et al." **Cycling is not a focus of Garmin Coach.** Free with Garmin Connect.
- **Not a direct competitor for cycling** (product gap).

### Strava Premium
- **Source:** https://www.strava.com/premium
- **Strava itself does not prescribe workouts.** It focuses on analysis, segments, routes. Prescription comes through the bundle with Runna (already mapped in the original scan). Strava individual: R$ 12.49/month; Strava + Runna bundle: R$ 29.17/month.
- **Confirmation:** Strava is a social/analysis layer, not coaching. Does not change the analysis.

### BestBikeSplit
- **Source:** https://www.bestbikesplit.com
- Exists. Primarily **race prediction / pacing** (physics + course + weather + power profile, 2-3% accuracy). **Relevant new fact:** now has an "AI Workout Builder" and "AI Assistant" on the premium tier — indicating expansion into prescription. Free tier (1 race / 1 bike), Premium ~US$ 10/month. Clientele includes WorldTour teams (EF, Trek, Canyon//SRAM).
- **Audience:** cyclists / triathletes with a specific target race.
- **Differentiation:** physics-based race modeling is unique in the market — a feature **none of the platforms mapped originally have** (not even TrainingPeaks). If a new platform wants to differentiate on "gran fondo / A-race", integrating race modeling is a real gap.

### Precision Fuel & Hydration
- **Source:** https://www.precisionhydration.com
- **Does not offer training plans**, only fueling / hydration. Free nutrition planning tool. Sells products (gels, drinks, tablets).
- **Out of the competitive scope**, but relevant as a future partnership / integration angle: fueling is not a feature of any of the platforms mapped originally.

### Final Surge
- **Source:** https://www.finalsurge.com
- Exists and is an **important TrainingPeaks-like competitor that was missing** from the original scan. Strong differentiator: **truly complete free tier — no ads, no in-app charges, no limits** ("Free from Ads / Free from Charges / Free from Limits"). Auto-sync with Garmin/Strava/TrainerRoad/Zwift/Polar/Rouvy/Stryd/Apple Watch. Supports running / cycling / swimming. Coach-athlete tooling (calendar, workout builder, communication). No mention of AI / adaptation.
- **Audience:** athletes + coaches (dual audience), including high school / collegiate / clubs.
- **Differentiation:** genuinely complete free tier (the market assumes paid).
- **Confirms gap:** focus on "container" for a human coach, not AI — reinforces that the "digitalized human coach" segment is sizeable.

### RideWithGPS
- **Source:** https://ridewithgps.com
- Focus on **route planning / navigation**, not coaching. No training plan feature identified.
- **Out of scope.**

### CoachCat
- **Source:** https://coach.cat
- **Persistent 500 error.** Not confirmed as active. Discard as a current reference.

### Trainiac
- **Source:** https://trainiac.com
- The primary domain returns a corporate e-learning consultancy, not fitness. If a "Trainiac coaching" ever existed in fitness, it apparently pivoted or was discontinued.
- **Out of scope.**

### Pillar
- Both pillar.app and pillar.co are unavailable (domains for sale).
- **Not relevant.**

### Spoked / MyPursuit
- Not confirmed via plausible domains (ECONNREFUSED / default Hostinger page).
- Do not include without further verification.

## Synthesis — new signals vs original scan

### Features / patterns that appear here and were not in the original 10

1. **Physics-based race modeling (BestBikeSplit).** Nobody in the original scan predicts a race split with real physics (course + weather + CdA + power profile). If a new platform targets amateur gran fondo, **this is a concrete differentiation gap available**.
2. **Truly complete free tier (Final Surge).** Opposite of the "14-day trial" default. Shows a segment that accepts payment only via premium human coach on top of the platform.
3. **Post-session rebuild (TrainAsONE, running).** The real benchmark for "adaptive". If a cycling platform still re-plans only weekly, it is not adaptive by TrainAsONE's bar.
4. **Peloton IQ.** The first mass-market ML player that "builds your plan" natively. The threat of "AI-in-fitness commodity" reaches cycling indoor via Peloton rather than via startups.
5. **Explicit demographic segmentation (Rouvy Masters 50+).** No original-scan platform has plans by age group. Potential gap (amateur cycling has a large 45+ cohort).
6. **Pausable subscription + family account (FulGaz).** A commercial model that reduces churn — non-existent in the original 10.

### Gaps we identified — still true?

- **Binary RPE / lack of feedback nuance.** **Still true.** None of the new platforms collect structured qualitative feedback.
- **No "why this workout".** **Still true.** No official page mentions prescription explainability.
- **No cohort / social peer.** **Still true.** Zwift/Peloton have generic social features, not training cohort.
- **Adaptation to life events (travel, sleep, illness).** **Partially closed by TrainAsONE** in running (uses recovery scores). In cycling, the **gap remains**.
- **Integrated fueling.** **Still true.** Precision does not do plans; no training platform natively integrates fueling.

### Recommended adjustments to the differentiation analysis

- **Remove Today's Plan** from the competitor set (shut down).
- **Add Final Surge and BestBikeSplit** as secondary competitors (different angles: free-tier and race-modeling).
- **Peloton IQ** enters as a growing indirect competitor (mass-market AI threat).
- **TrainAsONE** should be cited explicitly as **the technical benchmark for "adaptive"** even though it is running — the product team should test it to calibrate expectations.
